<div class="tei-poem-static tei-modern">
<h2 class="tei-title">The Fortune of the Dice</h2>
<p class="tei-subtitle">(Modern English Translation)</p>

<div class="tei-stanza">
  <div class="tei-line">First my ignorance and my uncouthness</div>
  <div class="tei-line">unto all of you who want to know your fate</div>
  <div class="tei-line">By cast of dice impress on your hearts</div>
  <div class="tei-line">And by good will to satisfy others</div>
  <div class="tei-line"><span class="tei-line-num">5</span>Even though I don’t really know</div>
  <div class="tei-line">The ways of the world I’ve heard it said</div>
  <div class="tei-line">That not everyone can thrive at once</div>
</div>

<div class="tei-stanza">
  <div class="tei-line">(2) I pray to God that every person may cast</div>
  <div class="tei-line">Upon three dice Just what is in his heart</div>
  <div class="tei-line"><span class="tei-line-num">10</span>Whether he is reckless or steadfast</div>
  <div class="tei-line">That He might scorn or else harm</div>
  <div class="tei-line">He who is guilty To change his life</div>
  <div class="tei-line">Those who in truth have suffered many such rolls</div>
  <div class="tei-line">Might their fortune fall so they might learn</div>
</div>

<div class="tei-stanza">
  <div class="tei-line"><span class="tei-line-num">15</span>(3) Since fortune governs all things</div>
  <div class="tei-line">Whatever happens, pardon me</div>
  <div class="tei-line">For I am not worthy to bear responsibility</div>
  <div class="tei-line">Or thanks, really, to any degree</div>
  <div class="tei-line">But nevertheless, I’ll say this for myself</div>
  <div class="tei-line"><span class="tei-line-num">20</span>She who can best help you in this need</div>
  <div class="tei-line">To roll right well I pray Fortune speeds you</div>
</div>

<p class="tei-trailer">End of Translation Fragment</p>
</div>
