        <h2 class="tei-title">The Chaunce of the Dyse</h2>

        <h2 class="tei-title">The Fortune of the Dice</h2>
        <p class="tei-subtitle">(Modern English Translation)</p>

        <div class="tei-stanza">
          <div class="tei-line">First my ignorance and my uncouthness</div>
          <div class="tei-line">unto all of you who want to know your fate</div>
          <div class="tei-line">By cast of dice impress on your hearts</div>
          <div class="tei-line">And by good will to satisfy others</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Even though I don’t really know</div>
          <div class="tei-line">The ways of the world I’ve heard it said</div>
          <div class="tei-line">That not everyone can thrive at once</div>
        </div>

        <div class="tei-stanza">
          <div class="tei-line">(2) I pray to God that every person may cast</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Upon three dice Just what is in his heart</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Whether he is reckless or steadfast</div>
          <div class="tei-line">That He might scorn or else harm</div>
          <div class="tei-line">He who is guilty To change his life</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Those who in truth have suffered many such rolls</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Might their fortune fall so they might learn</div>
        </div>

        <div class="tei-stanza">
          <div class="tei-line">Since fortune governs all things</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Whatever happens, pardon me</div>
          <div class="tei-line">For I am not worthy to bear responsibility</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Or thanks really to any degree</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Nevertheless, I’ll say this for myself</div>
          <div class="tei-line">She who can best help you in this need</div>
          <div class="tei-line">To roll right well I pray Fortune speeds you</div>
        </div>

        <div class="tei-stanza">
          <div class="tei-line">(4) As the oyster is wetnurse to the Orient pearl</div>
          <div class="tei-line">So you have been with virtue to the letter</div>
          <div class="tei-line">As clear as beryl in your chastity</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Like cinnamon which makes odors sweet</div>
          <div class="tei-line">So your humility all vice relieves</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Who pleynly knoweth / youre condicioun</div>
          <div class="tei-line">To yow may be made / no comparisoun</div>
        </div>

        <div class="tei-stanza">
          <div class="tei-line">(5) Even thinking about someone so two-faced</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Made my hand ashamed now I can’t write</div>
          <div class="tei-line">And honestly I can assure you</div>
          <div class="tei-line">My tonge ne may / sufficen for to endyte</div>
          <div class="tei-line">ffor vnto love / ye do so grete despite</div>
          <div class="tei-line">There Jason falseth oon / ye falsen twoo</div>
          <div class="tei-line">God willing I never hear about you again</div>
        </div>

        <div class="tei-stanza">
          <div class="tei-line">(6) The high renown of your lust for life</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Your steadfastness and your veracity</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Causes everyone who would put an honest effort</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Into learning nobility so as to avoid sloth</div>
          <div class="tei-line">To desire to be with you but to their great pity</div>
          <div class="tei-line">You should live without company</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Since your bounty is to avoid all folly</div>
        </div>

        <div class="tei-stanza">
          <div class="tei-line">(7) You cast fiercely with good acting</div>
          <div class="tei-line">It’s no wonder since you’ve been so bold</div>
          <div class="tei-line">Both disrespectful and stupid</div>
          <div class="tei-line">That with your words you so </div>
          <div class="tei-line">What man alive would trust or believe you</div>
          <div class="tei-line">And yet when dancing you trip</div>
          <div class="tei-line">you go up and down and don’t know which is which</div>
        </div>
