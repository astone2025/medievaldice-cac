This repository serves as a prototype for a digital critical edition of the medieval dice-poem _The Chaunce of the Dyse_. 
The transcription of the Middle English poem relies upon Eleanor Prescott Hammond's 1925 edition of the same title. That publication is now in the public domain, but difficult to find and only marginally annotated.
This digital edition seeks to make up for the lack of digitization of medieval participatory texts, as well as present the only modern english translation of the poem to date. 
My hope with this edition is to intrigue the casual reader and engage the practiced scholar.
